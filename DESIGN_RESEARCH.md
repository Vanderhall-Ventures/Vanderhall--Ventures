# Vanderhall Ventures: research-informed website refresh

Reviewed November 3, 2026. These are observed presentation patterns from established public brands, not verified revenue/conversion benchmarks. The research does not establish anyone's personal wealth or promise results for VV.

| Reference | Observed pattern | VV application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ATHLEAN-X, https://athleanx.com/the-training/all-programs | Scannable duration, weekly frequency, session length, product focus and purchase routes | Surface the existing 8-week / 4-day / 60-75 minute commitment near the paid decision |
| Mind Pump, https://www.mindpumpmedia.com/ | Founder explanation, video, testimonials, community personality and free educational material | Move Joseph's actual introduction video into the hero; retain the other founder video, client media and existing feed |
| Nerd Fitness, https://www.nerdfitness.com/ | Welcoming positioning and help choosing a starting point | Add a three-choice guide for restart, structured program, or custom plan; no personal-data collection |
| Acquisition.com, https://www.acquisition.com/training | Useful free resources, clear audience/problem matching, direct course actions | Give the free five-day reset one clear primary route; keep the eight-week program the core paid offer |

## Direction

Retain VV's original black/charcoal background, red glow, gold accents, bold typography, rounded panels and real videos. Refine hierarchy, spacing, texture and contrast. Original headline: “Build strength. Own your discipline.” Supporting line: “A stronger routine starts with a plan you can follow.”

Structure: founder-video hero; Train / Connect / Grow / Serve ribbon; optional starting-point guide; concise reset/build/connect process; three primary offers; actual client media and feed; founder background; paid-program details; community; optional e-book/customization; practical FAQs; closing free-reset action.

The e-book and custom blueprint remain available but do not compete as required intermediate purchases. The member app is in navigation for existing members. Two original YouTube videos, eight reel links, and the Curator feed remain. A mobile bottom action appears only after the hero leaves the viewport.

## Claims and measurement

Do not invent member counts, testimonials, credentials, revenue, urgency, discounts, endorsement badges or guaranteed transformations. Current price and time-commitment information is retained from the existing reviewed offers. Checkout remains authoritative. The starting-point guide provides deterministic recommendations, not medical or fitness suitability screening. It sends only a fixed path label as an optional click event.

The five-day companion remains browser-local. This refresh adds no central enrollment/check-in collection and no purchase verification. Evaluate visits, outbound clicks and actual provider/check-in records separately, as described in GROWTH_OPERATIONS.md. No conversion uplift has been established; establish a baseline and compare comparable cohorts after launch.

## Publication checks

Desktop and phone-width visual checks; readable content without horizontal overflow; menu and Escape behavior; each selector option updates its recommendation/link; two original video embeds; original offer/contact/member/policy routes; local assets/anchors/unique IDs; JavaScript syntax; reduced-motion styling. No paid checkout transaction is performed as part of this design refresh.
