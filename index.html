Mind Over Muscle — the 8-week program

Part of the Mental Edge experience. Eight weeks • four training days per week • 60–75 minutes per session.

Program breakdown

A structured weekly split with progressive reps, rest, tempo, and intent.

  • Weeks 1–2: Foundations & discipline setup
  • Weeks 3–4: Strength, structure, consistency
  • Weeks 5–6: Mental conditioning + performance habits
  • Weeks 7–8: Execution phase + ownership standards

Includes warm-up and cool-down protocols, an exercise substitution guide, weekly training and mindset check-in worksheets, the Weeks 5–8 Execution Bridge, and a completion certificate available as a separate download.

Know what to expect

The digital program is $27 USD as a one-time purchase. Review current delivery and refund terms on Gumroad before checkout.

  • Plan for four sessions of 60–75 minutes each week.
  • Use the weekly worksheets to reflect and plan your next week.
  • Ask about the custom blueprint if this schedule does not fit your needs.
  • Individual coaching is a separate service.

FAQ

Practical answers so you can choose your next step with confidence.

What’s the difference between Mental Edge and Mind Over Muscle?

Vanderhall Ventures is the main brand. Mental Edge is its training and accountability experience. Mind Over Muscle is the eight-week program, with a separate optional e-book available.

How do I get the guide or paid program?

The reset is a downloadable guide available on Gumroad with a zero-price option. Follow the download and access instructions supplied with your order. Keep your receipt and contact Joseph if access is missing. Existing members can use the Open App link.

What if the program does not fit my schedule?

The paid program uses four sessions of 60–75 minutes each week. Review the details before buying. If you need a different schedule or individual plan, ask about the custom blueprint.

Is my five-day checklist shared with my coach?

No. The optional progress companion stores five checkmarks in this browser only. It does not create an account, record a signup, or send completion data to Joseph.

How do I contact you?

DM @vanderhall_ventures or email joseph@vanderhallventuresllc.com.
