0 of 5 actions completed

DAY 01 / MAKE ROOM

Choose your training windows.

On your own calendar or paper, choose realistic training times for next week. Start from your actual responsibilities and available time.

DAY 02 / REMOVE FRICTION

Prepare for one obstacle.

Name the barrier most likely to stop you. Set out your gear, choose a location, or decide what a shorter session could look like.

DAY 03 / TAKE ACTION

Practice showing up.

Choose movement appropriate to your current ability and circumstances. Record what you did in your guide or on paper and notice what made it easier to begin.

DAY 04 / PLAN YOUR RETURN

Write your restart rule.

Finish this sentence on paper: “If I miss a session, my next step will be…” Make the response specific and realistic.

DAY 05 / KEEP BUILDING

Plan the week ahead.

Review what worked. Put your next sessions on the calendar and decide what support would help you follow through.
